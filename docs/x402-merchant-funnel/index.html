02 / WHAT NUMBERS MEAN

Do not collapse the payment state machine

Request discovery

sample and quote requests may be bots and crawlers. A quoted HTTP 402 alone is not a sale. Rate limits or bad schemas can stop a request before a paid challenge.

Willing to attempt payment

signed_retry in a merchant's own log is a stronger signal, but without facilitator acceptance, correct chain/token/recipient, distinct onchain Transfer and delivery it is not proof of purchase.

Possible paid outcome

settled + delivered rows are still self-reported. Confirm a unique chain:txHash:logIndex independently, then reconcile with the verified paid response, order and legitimate buyer before recording revenue.
