Tradeoffs stated plainly

Small structural checks, not a universal validator.

CSV validation checks shape, missing values, duplicate rows, suspected spreadsheet formulas, and common type-format anomalies. It cannot prove that statements are factually correct, scan for malware, determine financial accuracy or certify privacy compliance.

Never submit passwords, patient data, private customer records or confidential material. The application avoids persisting CSV cells or returning raw rows, but network providers, hosting and external payment facilitators have their own logging and privacy practices. Free examples are synthetic.
