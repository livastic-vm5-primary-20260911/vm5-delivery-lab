Deliberate limits protect your accounting judgement

Token explorer ≠ invoice

The receipt CSV can be forged. We do not make RPC requests, inspect signatures, confirm token ownership, reconcile internal wallet transfers, or verify that an event settled on the chain. The Base token address field is compared with the canonical Circle contract string only.

Conversion quote ≠ historical FX source

Arithmetic checks are on *reported* gross EUR, fees, and net EUR; no implied ECB/interbank rate, fair value, spread benchmark, custody treatment or P&L is invented. Original exchange confirmations and accounting policy remain necessary.

EUR credit ≠ bank confirmation

reported_posted is merely an input label, not bank access. Pooled exchange credits, partial offramps, unrelated wallet receipts, chargebacks, netting and multiple conversions per transaction require explicit allocation and manual review.
